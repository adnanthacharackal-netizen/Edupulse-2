# Google Play Store Listing & Launch Kit for EduPulse

Use this ready-to-paste content when publishing EduPulse on the **Google Play Console**.

---

## 1. Store Metadata

- **App Name:** EduPulse - Student Hub
- **Short Description (80 chars max):**  
  All-in-one student productivity, assignment deadlines, alarms & timetable app.
- **Category:** Education / Productivity
- **Content Rating:** Everyone (3+)
- **Primary Language:** English (United States)
- **Application ID:** `com.edupulse.studenthub`

---

## 2. Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
EduPulse is the ultimate academic productivity and student dashboard app designed specifically for college students, university scholars, and professors. Stay on top of coursework deadlines, organize class timetables, and never miss an assignment submission again!

✨ KEY FEATURES:

📅 1. INTERACTIVE ACADEMIC CALENDAR & TIMETABLE
• View daily class schedules with room numbers, instructor details, and subject codes.
• Track exam dates, project presentation sprints, and study review sessions.
• Seamlessly switch between clean Month Grid and Agenda List views.

⏰ 2. AUTOMATED DEADLINE ALARM SYSTEM
• Schedule real-time alerts before deadlines: 7 days, 3 days, 1 day, 2 hours, 30 minutes, or at the exact due time.
• Gentle audible chime and device notifications ensure you submit coursework on time.
• Automatic overdue alerts if a cutoff passes without submission.

✅ 3. ASSIGNMENT & COURSEWORK MANAGEMENT
• Organize coursework by subject, priority (Urgent, High, Medium, Low), and status.
• Live deadline countdowns ("Due in 2 days, 4 hours").
• Attach rubric links, submission notes, and professor instructions.
• Filter and sort by nearest deadline or highest priority.

📝 4. DAILY STUDY TASKS & TO-DO LISTS
• Break down exam prep into manageable daily tasks.
• Track your daily study completion rate with a dynamic progress bar.
• Categorize tasks by Study, Homework, Projects, Reading, or Revision.

📚 5. SUBJECT-WISE REVISION NOTES
• Capture lecture summaries, formula cheat sheets, and algorithm notes.
• Search notes instantly by keyword or course code.
• Pin essential study notes to the top of your revision list.

🌓 6. CLEAN MODERN DESIGN & DARK MODE
• High-contrast Dark Mode optimized for late-night campus study sessions.
• 100% offline-ready: all your data remains accessible even without Wi-Fi.

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST:
EduPulse respects student privacy. We never sell your personal data or track your browsing activity. Your academic records stay on your device or in your secure cloud account.

Download EduPulse today and take control of your academic semester!
```

## 3. Permissions Justification for Google Play Review

When Google Play requests explanations for sensitive permissions:

- `android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`:  
  *Justification:* "EduPulse is an academic deadline management application. Students require exact-time reminders prior to strict assignment cutoffs and exam times to prevent late submission academic penalties."
- `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`:  
  *Justification:* "Used to deliver critical assignment reminder notifications and exam alerts scheduled by the student."
